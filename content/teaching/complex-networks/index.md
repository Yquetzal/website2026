# Introduction
This is the page of the Complex Networks course at ENS de Lyon., in the Complex System option, for the year **2026/2027**

# Content
The class covers two main parts 

A) Fundamentals of Network Science -- static properties of graphs (Rémy Cazabet and Pierre Borgnat)
-  Classic random graph models
-  Definition and use of centrality metrics
-  Small-world phenomenon
-  Etc.

B) Dynamic processes on networks (Alexandre Nicolas)
- Microscopic rules on the network: from physical interactions to individual decisions
- Theoretical approaches on simple networks: mean-field approach, percolation, etc.
- Flow networks: notion of capacity constraint, user vs. system equilibrium, basics of game theory
- Stochastic dynamics on networks: transition matrix, equation of evolution, mixing time, spectral properties

  
## Objectives
This class is thought to provide a broad overview of current topics in Network Science. It is grounded in research.


## Overview of the course
There are 24h of lectures and 8h of practicals. The course is given in English, unless all students are French speakers.

## Exams

There will be two grades given in this class:

* A network analysis project.
Your report must be handed in before December 13 , 23h59. A short presentation, followed by questions, will take place in the afternoon of December, 17th (40%)
You can work in groups of 2 or 3 students. You must clarify what each student has done (grades can be personnalized).
You can find some examples of previous years' projects [there](https://cazabetremy.fr/Teaching/CN/example_projects), but this year you will be asked to favour open questions.

* A final Exam (60%). All documents allowed (no computers). This exam covers classes by all teachers. You can find an exam from a previous year [there](http://cazabetremy.fr/Teaching/CN/Exams_2022.pdf). Note that the program was not exactly the same, but it gives an idea of the kind of questions you can have on my part.
