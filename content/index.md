# Networks, communities & complex systems

I study how networks form, evolve and organise — from communities and social interactions to complex systems.

I am an associate professor at **[Sorbonne Université](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr)** and a member of the [Complex Networks](https://www.complexnetworks.fr) team at [LIP6](https://www.lip6.fr/). From September 2026 to September 2028, I am on secondment from my tenured position at **[Université Lyon 1](https://www.univ-lyon1.fr/en)**, held since 2017.

[Publications](https://cazabetremy.fr/?page=publications) · [Research projects](https://cazabetremy.fr/?page=projects) · [Teaching](https://cazabetremy.fr/?page=teaching)

## Research interests

- **Communities & network structure** — community detection, network embedding and temporal networks.
- **Social & economic networks** — online interactions, social network analysis and digital-currency transaction networks.
- **Complex systems & AI** — complex systems and LLM-driven multi-agent systems.

## Background

I previously held postdoctoral positions at [Sorbonne Université](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/en), [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/) and Japan’s [National Institute of Informatics](https://www.nii.ac.jp/en/). I obtained my PhD at [Toulouse University](https://en.univ-toulouse.fr).
